How accurate is it?

Errora uses the YouCam 2D Earring Virtual Try-On engine, which accepts a physical size in centimetres (earring_scale) and scales the earring to your ear in the photo. In our tests, sizes rendered in exact proportion: 1.5, 3 and 6 cm came out at a 1 : 2 : 4 ratio. Comparisons between sizes are therefore reliable. Absolute size assumes typical ear proportions, so treat it as a close approximation rather than a guarantee.
